Capabilities

Useful from first question to final handoff.

A focused set of capabilities for founders, product teams and studios that need senior-level product thinking without process theatre.

01 / DISCOVER

Product strategy

Frame the problem, identify constraints, map the opportunity and define what success should look like before execution.

02 / SHAPE

UX & interaction

Turn complex flows into interfaces that feel obvious, resilient and intentional across real states.

03 / SCALE

Design systems

Create reusable foundations, tokens and documentation that make quality faster instead of slower.

04 / PROVE

Prototyping & testing

Prototype at realistic density, test the riskiest assumptions and use evidence to decide what ships.

05 / SHIP

Design + engineering

Work close to implementation so feasibility is part of design, not a surprise after handoff.

06 / LEARN

Post-launch thinking

Define the metric, watch the outcome and turn launch data into the next product decision.

Process

Four beats. No theatre.

01

Name the constraint

Business, technical and human constraints get explicit before the first polished screen.

02

Explore the smallest useful version

Find the simplest interaction that can prove or disprove the risky assumption.

03

Bring engineering in early

Feasibility is discussed while ideas are cheap to change, not after a grand handoff.

04

Measure what shipped

Agree on the success signal in advance, then let the result shape the next iteration.
